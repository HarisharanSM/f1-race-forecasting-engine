# Mixed-Input Historical Backtest

Run `.venv/bin/python scripts/backtest_mixed_history.py` to evaluate 2010 through the saved 2026 results. Completed format reports can be resumed only when dataset checksums and training settings match. Reports are written to `artifacts/mixed-input-backtest-2010-2026`, leaving earlier experiments and deployed models unchanged.

Existing verified enriched Grand Prix and sprint snapshots are preserved. Missing Grand Prix sessions use classifications from the checksum-verified historical source collection, earlier driver/team result-based form and actual qualifying order for race predictions. Qualifying predictions do not receive their own classification as a feature. Target race laps, fastest laps, positions and incidents are not forecast inputs. Reconstructed final rosters remain a retrospective limitation.

Reduced inputs explicitly impute rain/wet fraction at 0.2, temperature at 25 C and wind at zero. Unsupported car and circuit traits retain model defaults. Feedback weather fields in reduced records are compatibility imputations, NOT measured or verified weather, even though the classification itself is verified. They condition training with the same fixed assumptions. No weather forecasting skill is evaluated. Missing session clock times use noon UTC; classifications are assumed available 12 hours later. These assumptions are not historical measurements. Race and qualifying rosters must match exactly; invalid classifications are excluded instead of inventing positions. Starting-grid penalties are not reconstructed for reduced sessions.

The field Transformer is trained afresh for each held-out weekend, with eight earlier Grand Prix training weekends and two validation weekends required; sprint uses its saved format-specific settings. Component configuration matches the primary architecture's legacy optional-input adapters, without learned measurement correction or pace calibration. Grand Prix and sprint are fitted separately. Initial weekends are warm-up, not scored tests. Probabilities and prediction intervals are uncalibrated; greater confidence is not automatically greater reliability.

The index separates reduced and richer-existing input coverage and every evaluated year. Richer-existing does not mean every optional telemetry, tyre, incident or upgrade input exists. Source acquisition is distinct from validated snapshot availability. The interactive report preserves the year/circuit/session/scenario workflow and labels missing-weather assumptions. The 2026 evaluation is year-to-date only. Overall metrics across this expanded era cannot establish improvement over earlier reports with different coverage.
